<h2>FAQ: Hard Water Stains Before Diwali</h2>

<h3>How do I deal with hard water stains before Diwali quickly?</h3>
<p>Focus on chrome taps, the shower head and ceramic tiles guests see. Use vinegar only on safe surfaces, rinse, dry, and photograph one spot so you can judge the result.</p>

<h3>What is the best diwali bathroom cleaning order?</h3>
<p>Ventilate, protect stone, soak the shower head, clean taps and aerators, do tiles and glass, wipe the toilet, then dry everything. Work top to bottom.</p>

<h3>Which diwali cleaning tips hard water homes should not skip?</h3>
<p>Do not mix bleach and acids, do not put vinegar on natural stone, and always dry chrome and glass after rinsing.</p>

<h3>Can I remove hard water stains for Diwali without vinegar?</h3>
<p>Yes. Use a commercial limescale remover labelled for your finish, or a stone-safe cleaner on natural stone. Follow the label.</p>
