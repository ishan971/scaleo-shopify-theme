<h2>Is Hard Water Harmful for Plants? White Crust and What to Do</h2>
<p>Is hard water harmful for plants in the sense of instant poison? Usually no. Harm shows as gradual stress: crust, tip burn, slow growth in containers, clogged drip points.</p>

<li>Scrape or flush surface crust in pots.</li>
<li>Improve drainage and stop water sitting in saucers.</li>
<li>Switch prized pots to rainwater or untreated outdoor water if available.</li>
<li>Do not "fix" crust by pouring softener salt water on plants.</li>

<h3>Is hard water bad for indoor plants specifically?</h3>
