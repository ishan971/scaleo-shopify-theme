## 7. Claims you must not write

- Medical claims: "cures dandruff", "treats eczema / psoriasis", "stops hair fall", "prevents kidney stones"
- "Removes hardness" or "makes water soft" in the ion-exchange sense. Prefer: conditions minerals, helps reduce new scale, salt-free tank insert
- "Lasts forever" or "zero maintenance". Truth: no salt, no power, no servicing; unit exchanged on a ~6-month cycle
- "Removes iron / rust / yellow colour" from borewell water
- "Descales your old geyser" (Scale-O slows **new** scale; old crust needs one manual descale)
- "Safe to drink / purifies water". Scale-O is not a drinking-water purifier
- Apartment / tap-level install as the default (Scale-O sits in an **overhead tank**)
- Attacking KENT, 3M, Zero B, or any polyphosphate seller by name in the **title**. Honest compare in the body is fine
- Unproven social proof ("trusted by 2L+ families")

Customer-facing copy: no em dashes.
