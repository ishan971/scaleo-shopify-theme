## Topic details

### O1. How to Clean a Shower Head Clogged by Hard Water (Thu 8 Oct)

- **URL:** `/blogs/articles/how-to-clean-shower-head`
- **Secondary keywords:** how to clean shower head holes; shower head clogged with mineral deposits; how to clean shower heads from hard water
- **What it covers:** Soak and unblock holes at home (on and off the arm), what is safe for rain and hand showers, why holes clog again in weeks on tank water, then tank-level treatment.
- **Why now:** Diwali cleaning searches peak in October. The live stains post covers taps and tiles, not blocked shower holes.

### O2. Whole House Water Softener for Independent Houses in India (Fri 9 Oct)

- **URL:** `/blogs/articles/whole-house-water-softener-india`
- **Secondary keywords:** water softener for independent house; water softener for villa; water softener for bungalow; water softener for house tank; water softener for Sintex tank
- **What it covers:** Three whole-house options (inlet salt plant, overhead tank insert, bathroom units) compared on install, power, salt, space, and yearly cost. Villas with 2+ tanks. Scale-O as the default for overhead-tank homes.
- **Why:** These searches describe Scale-O's exact buyer.

### O3. Geyser Descaling: How to Remove Scale Before Winter (Mon 12 Oct)

- **URL:** `/blogs/articles/geyser-descaling`
- **Secondary keywords:** how to descale geyser at home; geyser scale remover; geyser descaling cost; anti scale geyser
- **What it covers:** Signs a geyser needs descaling, safe steps, technician cost range, what "anti scale" geysers mean, then "descale once, protect after" with Scale-O.
- **Why now:** Geyser use starts with winter. The live geyser post covers damage, not the how-to and cost.

### O4 (NEW). Water Hardness Levels: What Your PPM or TDS Number Means (Tue 13 Oct)

- **URL:** `/blogs/articles/water-hardness-levels`
- **Secondary keywords:** hard water ppm range; water hardness levels chart; borewell water hardness level; TDS level for hard water; water hardness level for bathing
- **What it covers:** Simple chart of soft, moderate, hard and very hard levels. What a borewell or tanker reading means for taps, geyser and hair. Which Scale-O size suits which tank, with honest notes for very high TDS.
- **Why:** Google India autocomplete shows `water hardness levels` (ppm, chart, for bathing, for drinking), `hard water ppm` (range, chart, level) and `borewell water hardness` (level, limit, range, ppm). People search this right after testing their water, so it leads straight to buying.
- **Overlap check:** The live test post covers *how to test*. The old TDS post covers *TDS vs hardness*. This post covers *what your number means* and links to both.

### O5. Water Softener for Solar Water Heaters on Hard Water (Wed 14 Oct)

- **URL:** `/blogs/articles/water-softener-for-solar-water-heater`
- **Secondary keywords:** solar water heater for hard water India; solar water heater descaling; best solar water heater for hard water
- **What it covers:** How scale chokes ETC tubes and FPC panels, warning signs, descaling, and why the overhead tank (which feeds the solar heater) is the right place to treat.
- **Why:** Independent houses with terrace solar heaters are a near-perfect fit, with little competition.

### O6 (NEW). Hard Water vs Soft Water for Bathing: Hair, Skin and Daily Use (Thu 15 Oct)

- **URL:** `/blogs/articles/hard-water-vs-soft-water-for-bathing`
- **Secondary keywords:** hard water vs soft water for hair; hard water vs soft water for skin; hard water vs soft water which is better; soft water benefits for skin and hair
- **What it covers:** What changes in the shower: shampoo film, dry skin, scalp feel, white residue on hair. Salt-softened vs conditioned vs untreated tank water, and what Scale-O can and cannot change.
- **Why:** Google India autocomplete for `hard water vs soft water` returns for bathing, for skin, for hair and which is better.
- **Product caution:** Scale-O conditions water; it does not make it "soft" like a salt plant. The post says this clearly and makes no soft-water or medical promises. Links Blog 1 (hair fall), Blog 7 (skin) and O9 (dandruff) without taking their keywords.

**Spare topic (if the client rejects new O4 or O6):** *Water Softener for a New House* (`water softener for new home`; secondary: best water softener for new home, water softener for house tank).
