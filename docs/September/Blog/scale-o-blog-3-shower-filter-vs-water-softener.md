<h2>FAQ: Shower Filter vs Water Softener</h2>

<h3>Is a shower filter the same as a water softener?</h3>
<p>No. A shower filter or bathroom unit treats one outlet. A tank-level softener or conditioner treats water stored for the house line.</p>

<h3>Bathroom water softener vs whole house: which should I buy?</h3>
<p>If you control an overhead tank and multiple fixtures show scale, buy whole-house tank treatment. Use a bathroom unit only when tank access is impossible or you need a temporary single-outlet fix.</p>

<h3>Will a shower filter protect my geyser?</h3>
<p>No. The geyser heats tank water that never passed through the shower attachment.</p>

<h3>Can I use both a shower filter and Scale-O?</h3>
<p>You can, but it is usually unnecessary once tank-level treatment is sized correctly. Fix the tank first, then decide if any point-of-use add-on is still worth it.</p>
