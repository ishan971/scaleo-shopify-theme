Each keyword had to pass **at least three live signals** on **9 September 2026**:

| Signal | What was used | Why it counts as research |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Google India autocomplete** | `suggestqueries.google.com` with `gl=in`, `hl=en` | Google only suggests queries people in India already type |
| **2. Live SERP / competitor content** | KENT, Hard2Soft, RiverSoft, WaterScience, Pearl Water | If a competitor funds a dedicated URL, demand is real |
| **3. Demand geography** | CGWB Annual Groundwater Quality Report 2024 (15,259 samples); PIB / CGWB hardness maps | City blogs only where hardness is documented, not random cities |
| **4. Category context** | IMARC: India water softener market ~USD 119.4M in 2026; salt-based still ~79% share; salt-free is the niche Scale-O can win | Confirms commercial intent exists; does not replace keyword volume |
| **5. Cannibalization audit** | Live scale-o.com blog index + city pages + collection/PDP primaries from the Aug SEO plan | Stops two URLs fighting |

## 7. Claims you must not write

- Medical hair-growth, eczema treatment, or “stops balding”
- “Removes hardness” / “makes water soft like rain” if you mean ion-exchange
- Prefer: conditions minerals, helps reduce scale, salt-free tank insert
- “Trusted by 2L+ families” unless proven
- Apartment / tap-level install as the default (Scale-O sits in an **overhead tank**)
- Recreating August primaries on new URLs
- Attacking KENT or Hard2Soft by name in the **title**. Honest compare in the body is fine
- Soap lather as a promised Scale-O result (chelators usually do not restore lather like salt plants)

Customer-facing copy: no em dashes.
