<h2>Daily water use vs tank litres (the part buyers skip)</h2>
<p>Tank stickers are easy. Daily use is fuzzy, so people ignore it. Do not.</p>
<p>A water softener for overhead tank duty sits in stored water that keeps refreshing. High daily draw means more hard water enters, mixes, and leaves toward fixtures. Low daily draw means the same stored volume turns over slowly. That is why two homes with the same printed tank size can need different attention in real life.</p>
<p>Practical cues of high daily draw:</p>
<ul>
<li>Multiple morning showers overlapping</li>
<li>Washing machine most days</li>
<li>Floor cleaning and outdoor tap use from the same tank line</li>
<li>Frequent tanker or borewell top-ups because the tank empties fast</li>
</ul>
<p>If those cues stack on a mid or large tank, prefer Semi or Centi per the table rather than “buying the cheapest Mini because the house is not that big.” Floor area is not tank chemistry.</p>

<p>That does not mean you rebuy a new model every month. It means:</p>
<ul>
<li>Size for your harder usual pattern and tank band</li>
<li>Retest with a boil check when scale suddenly spikes after a source change</li>
<li>Keep expectations honest: a salt-free insert conditions minerals in the tank water you have now, not last year’s lab report from a different locality</li>

<h2>What a tank softener will not do</h2>
<p>Clear limits protect trust.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Not a replacement for RO drinking water</strong> if your household already relies on RO for drinking and cooking taste or safety habits.</li>
<li><strong>Not ion-exchange softening.</strong> Do not expect salt-plant strip readings or big soap-lather changes.</li>
<li><strong>Not a shower filter alternative for apartments without a tank.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Not an overnight museum cleanup.</strong> Old scale on fittings may need one mechanical clean; the unit’s job is reducing new hard deposits from tank-fed water.</li>
<li><strong>Not a medical hair treatment.</strong> Hard water can worsen breakage feel for some people; that is not the same as a clinical hair-growth claim. For breakage vs shedding language, see the hair guide on the blog when that is your main question.</li>

<h2>First 30 days after you drop a unit in the tank</h2>
<p>Treat the first month like a field notebook, not a magic reveal day.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Day 0:</strong> Photo the kettle interior, one tap base, and the shower rose. Note geyser heat-up feel in plain words.</li>
<li><strong>Week 1:</strong> Confirm the unit is still submerged and stable after fills.</li>
<li><strong>Week 2 to 4:</strong> Compare new crust speed on the same kettle or tap you photographed. Look for slower return of white film after cleaning, not a brand-new bathroom.</li>
<li><strong>If nothing changes at all:</strong> recheck size vs tank litres, confirm the unit is in the tank that actually feeds the problem fixtures, and re-run a hard water screen. Wrong tank or wrong size is more common than “all hard water is identical.”</li>
</ol>
<p>Keep RO habits unchanged unless a water specialist told you otherwise for drinking water. Tank-level scale control and drinking RO are different layers of the house.</p>

<p>The best overhead tank water softener is the one matched to your tank litres and daily draw. For Scale-O’s salt-free line, that means Mini, Semi, or Centi sized to the bands above, not a bathroom-only filter marketed as whole-house.</p>

<h3>Is a water softener for overhead tank better than a shower filter?</h3>
<p>For whole-house scale on tank-fed plumbing, yes: tank-level treats the shared source. A shower filter only changes one outlet.</p>

<h3>Which model for a water softener for 1000 litre tank?</h3>

<h3>Do I need a plumber or electricity?</h3>
<p>Not for a Scale-O drop-in overhead tank water softener. You need safe tank access and correct sizing.</p>

<h3>Will this remove the need for RO?</h3>
<p>No. Keep RO for drinking if that is your household practice. Tank inserts and RO solve different jobs.</p>

<h3>Salt-free or salt-based for an overhead tank terrace?</h3>
